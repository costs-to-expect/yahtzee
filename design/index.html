Colour

A deep teal for every scorer. A game is told apart by its mark and its name, not its colour, so adding Scrabble or Carcassonne never means a new palette. Costs to Expect purple is kept for the places the app speaks as Costs to Expect.

  • 700 is the working colour: buttons, links and text on white or paper (5.8:1).
  • 800 is the hover for 700 and text on the pale tints, never lighter on hover.
  • 50 and 100 are tints for soft buttons, selected rows and the leader’s tile.
  • 600 and lighter are for rings, bars and fills only, white text on 600 is 4:1.

Costs to Expect purple

The footer on every page and the account pages, nowhere else. It sits 120° from teal and from gold on the colour wheel, so the three belong together.

Gold, for winning

A crown for who is leading now, a trophy for who won a finished game. Never used for warnings, those are amber with an alert icon and words.

Status

Saved Not saved Delete

Green, amber and red are only ever status, teal is never one of them. Colour is always backed by an icon and words.

Players

Six soft colours, none of them teal or gold. A player gets theirs from their place in the players list, so they keep it in every game and there is nothing to store.

Type

Figtree, self-hosted. Friendly without being childish, and it has real tabular figures, so a column of scores lines up and a total never jiggles as it changes.
